## 7. Slide-by-Slide Writing Technique (How to Keep Viewers Swiping)

To ensure users swipe from Slide 1 all the way to Slide 6 (giving you high algorithmic retention):

1. **Slide 1 (Hook):** Keep it under 10–12 words. Big bold font. Add a visual arrow `➔` at bottom right.
2. **Slide 2 (The Friction):** Describe the exact scene that happens in their living room. Use words like *"તમે જ્યારે...", "ત્યારે સામેથી..."*.
3. **Slide 3 (The Psychology):** Validate them! Don't judge. Use psychological terms simply: *"આ અહંકાર નથી, પણ નર્વસ સિસ્ટમનું શટડાઉન છે."*
4. **Slide 4 (The Wake-up Call):** Show what happens if they don't fix it (especially to children or long-term happiness).
5. **Slide 5 (The 1-2-3 Formula):** Numbered cards with actionable scripts. High save trigger.
6. **Slide 6 (The Outro / CTA):** Never just say "Follow me." Give an exact trigger keyword.

## 9. Visual & Design Standards for 10x Better Views

1. **Color Palette:** Avoid loud neon colors (screaming red/yellow). Use **Warm Luxury & Emotional Healing tones**:
   * Background: Warm Sand / Cream (`#FBF9F5`) or Deep Slate Taupe (`#2C2B29`)
   * Accent: Terracotta (`#C86D51`) or Warm Amber Gold (`#D4A373`)
   * Text: Charcoal Dark (`#1E1E1E`) or Crisp Ivory
2. **Aspect Ratio:** Always **4:5 vertical (1080 x 1350 px)**. Never post 1:1 square; 4:5 occupies 20% more screen space on mobile feed, preventing fast scrolling.
3. **Typography:** Maximum 2 fonts. One clean bold Sans-Serif for Headlines (Noto Sans Gujarati / Inter) and a clean regular font for body.
4. **Clean Margins:** Keep 100px padding around all sides so Instagram UI buttons (Like, Share, Bookmark) do not cover your text.
