# Requirements: Online Quiz & Digital Certificate System

## 1. Course & Assessment Specs
- **Course Name**: Child Psychology & Development – Basic Awareness
- **Duration**: 10 Minutes countdown
- **Total Questions**: 10 multiple-choice questions
- **Grading**: 1 mark per correct answer, 0 for incorrect/unanswered
- **Passing Criteria**: 50% (Minimum 5 out of 10 marks)
- **Fee**: ₹250 (Non-formal educational awareness program)

## 2. Security & Eligibility Rules
- **Non-Formal Disclaimer**: Prominently stated across registration, quiz, and certificate.
- **Server-Side Grading**: Quiz answers graded on server; clients cannot spoof marks.
- **Payment Verification**: Sandbox/Test environment support. Unpaid/failed attempts cannot take quiz or claim certificates.
- **Certificate Eligibility Gate**: Score >= 5/10, verified payment, valid user registration, single certificate per attempt.
- **Watermark & Test Isolation**: Watermarked "TEST PREVIEW – NOT VALID FOR OFFICIAL USE"; separate registry for test vs official records.
- **Admin Dashboard**: View registrations, quiz scores, payment states, and inspect test certificate previews.
