# Project: Online Quiz and Digital Certificate System

## Overview
Comprehensive online quiz, sandbox payment, automated scoring, server-side eligibility verification, and digital certificate generation system for non-formal educational awareness programs (specifically "Child Psychology & Development – Basic Awareness").

## Goals
- Provide an immersive, onboarding-like experience with glassmorphism / liquid glass UI.
- Implement strict server-side grading, tamper-proof score validation, and 10-minute timer enforcement.
- Support test/sandbox payment verification (₹250 course fee) preventing unverified access.
- Generate high-fidelity test certificate previews with black-and-gold styling, unique IDs (`TEST-CPD-XXXX`), watermarks, and non-formal disclaimers.
- Integrate test audit records into the Admin Dashboard.
- Prevent any official certificate issuance without explicit administrative authorization.
